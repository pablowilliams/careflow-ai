# DPIA starter

CareFlow AI uses synthetic data only. This document demonstrates the questions and evidence expected before any real processing; it is not legal advice or an approved DPIA.

## Proposed processing

The target concept would read an assigned referral, apply administrative completeness rules, retrieve approved pathway information, create a draft communication, and record an attributable human decision. It explicitly excludes diagnosis, clinical urgency, treatment recommendation, automated decision-making, and autonomous communication.

## Questions requiring accountable answers

- What exact operational purpose and lawful basis apply?
- Is AI necessary and proportionate compared with deterministic automation?
- Which controller, processor, subprocessors, regions, retention periods, and contractual controls apply?
- Which fields are strictly required, and which can be removed or pseudonymized?
- How will transparency, access, correction, objection, and other rights be supported?
- How are confidentiality, special-category data, model-provider data use, and international transfer managed?
- What consultation with affected staff and patients is proportionate?
- What residual high risks remain and who accepts or escalates them?

## POC controls

Synthetic records, purpose limitation, approved retrieval, no model training, exact-payload approval, role separation, redacted observability, time-limited state, deletion path, and explicit production block.
