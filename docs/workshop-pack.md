# Discovery workshop pack

## Decision

Should the organization progress a bounded referral-administration assistant to a synthetic alpha, and what evidence is required before a controlled beta?

## Ninety-minute agenda

1. 0-10: outcome, scope, and decision.
2. 10-25: map current referral journey and handoffs.
3. 25-40: quantify pain, variability, and baseline.
4. 40-55: identify data, integrations, and authoritative policy.
5. 55-70: surface hazards, misuse, and unacceptable automation.
6. 70-82: compare deterministic, assistant, hybrid, and no-change options.
7. 82-90: decisions, evidence gaps, owners, and next checkpoint.

## Required participants

SRO, service manager, referral coordinators, clinical reviewer, Clinical Safety Officer, DPO/IG, security, enterprise/data/API architects, engineering/SRE, product/design, and procurement/commercial.

## Outputs

Current-state blueprint, baseline, stakeholder/RACI map, data-flow sketch, AI use-case card, red-line list, initial hazards, option assessment, value hypothesis, and ninety-day evidence plan.
